# AI CFO Copilot

## Vision

AI CFO Copilot is an AI-powered Finance Operating System designed for SMEs and Mid-Market businesses.

The platform enables finance teams to complete the entire month-end close process from one application.

Instead of using multiple spreadsheets, BI tools and disconnected software, AI CFO Copilot becomes the central finance workspace for reporting, forecasting, benchmarking, AI insights and statutory compliance.

---

# Mission

Reduce month-end reporting from days to minutes while providing CFO-quality insights to every business.

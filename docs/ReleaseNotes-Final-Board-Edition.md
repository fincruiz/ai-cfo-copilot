# AI CFO Copilot - Board Intelligence Edition

## New flagship capabilities

- Board & Management Reporting Studio with a five-stage guided workflow.
- Interactive collection of strategic, operational, governance and workforce inputs.
- Workforce mix inputs for technical, management, apprentice, sales and administration staff.
- Editable branch workforce allocation table.
- Dedicated Ratio & Productivity Centre.
- Automated DSO, DPO, DIO, cash conversion cycle, current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio, debt-to-equity, debt-to-assets, margins, returns, asset turnover and employee productivity ratios.
- AI-supported executive board narrative using financial results, ratios, workforce data, risks, priorities and management outlook.
- Editable narrative before publication.
- Downloadable Word and HTML board reports.
- Board pack readiness indicator and explicit management validation warning.

## Important

The generated report is management-prepared and AI-assisted. Financial mapping, period assumptions, average balances, narrative and sensitive disclosures must be validated before circulation.

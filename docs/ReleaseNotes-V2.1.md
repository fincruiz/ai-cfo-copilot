# AI CFO Copilot Enterprise UI V2.1

## Corrections

- Forced the desktop navigation sidebar to remain mounted and visible after page navigation and Streamlit reruns.
- Removed sidebar collapse controls from the enterprise shell.
- Rebalanced the dark theme from near-black to a lighter slate/navy palette for better readability during long finance sessions.
- Replaced the bottom-right circular AI button with an animated right-edge Copilot tab on desktop.
- Positioned the AI drawer beside the Copilot tab and vertically centred it.
- Preserved a compact bottom launcher on smaller screens.
- Increased card, panel, top-bar and navigation contrast without changing finance calculations or reporting logic.

## Installation

Extract the package, copy your existing `.streamlit/secrets.toml` and `.venv` into the project folder, then launch `start.bat`.
